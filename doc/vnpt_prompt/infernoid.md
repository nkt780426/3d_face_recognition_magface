  - **Reveal 1 "**[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster or 1 "[Void](https://cardcluster.com/search/Void)" Spell/Trap in your hand; discard your entire hand, then draw the same number of cards you discarded. **If you control no monsters, or all monsters you control are Fiend monsters, except the turn this card was sent to the GY:**You can banish this card from your GY, then target up to 11 of your banished "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters with different names; return them to the GY. You can only use this effect of "Void Reignition" once per Duel.
- Void Unleashing: 3

  - **Reveal 1 "**[Shaddoll](https://cardcluster.com/search/Shaddoll)" card in your hand; add 2 "[Shaddoll](https://cardcluster.com/search/Shaddoll)" cards from your Deck to your hand, each with a different type (Monster, Spell, or Trap) from the revealed card and from each other, then discard 1 card. **You can banish this card from your GY;** add 1 "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster from your Deck or GY to your hand. You can only use each effect of "Void Unleashing" once per turn.
- Void Expansion: 1

  - **During your Standby Phase:** You can Special Summon 1 "[Infernoid Token](<https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid%20Token>)" (Fiend-Type/FIRE/Level 1/ATK 0/DEF 0). To Special Summon an "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster using its own procedure, you can banish "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster(s) you control instead of those in the Graveyard (or any combination of both). Your opponent cannot target "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters you control for attacks or with card effects, except the "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster you control with the highest Level (either, if tied).
- Void Apocalypse: 3

  - **You can discard 1 card;** send 1 Fiend monster from your Deck to the GY. **You can send this face-up card from your Spell & Trap Zone to the GY;** send "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters with different names from your hand and/or Deck to the GY, up to the number of monsters your opponent controls that were Special Summoned from the Extra Deck. You can only use 1 "Void Apocalypse" effect per turn, and only once that turn.
- Void Breach: 3

  - "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters you control gain 100 ATK for each of your banished "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters. **If you control no monsters, or all monsters you control are Fiend monsters:**You can activate 1 of these effects;
    ●Add 1 of your banished "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters to your hand.
    ●Special Summon 1 "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster from your hand, ignoring its Summoning conditions.
    You can only use this effect of "Void Breach" once per turn.
- Void Imagination: 3

  - "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters you control whose original Levels are 2 or higher become Level 1 and all battle damage they inflict to your opponent is halved. **You can send this card to the GY;** Fusion Summon 1 "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" Fusion Monster from your Extra Deck, using monsters from your hand or field as material. If your opponent controls a monster that was Special Summoned from the Extra Deck, and you do not, you can also use up to 6 monsters in your Deck as Fusion Materials.
- Void Vanishment: 3

  - **You can discard 1 card;** add 1 "[Void](https://cardcluster.com/search/Void)" Spell/Trap from your Deck to your hand, except "Void Vanishment" or "[Null and Void](<https://cardcluster.com/search/Null%20and%20Void>)", also you cannot Normal or Special Summon for the rest of this turn, except "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monsters. You can only use this effect of "Void Vanishment" once per turn. **If an "**[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster you control battles an opponent's monster, after damage calculation:**You can send this card to the GY;** banish those monsters.
- Void Seer: 1

  - **Target 1 "**[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster you control; that target is unaffected by the opponent's card effects this turn. If an "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster(s) you control would be destroyed by a card effect, you can banish this card from your Graveyard instead of destroying 1 of those monsters.
- Eye of the Void: 1

  - Special Summon 1 "[Infernoid](https://cardcluster.com/search/Infernoid)" monster from your hand, ignoring its Summoning conditions, but its effects are negated until the end of this turn

- Void Launch: 1
